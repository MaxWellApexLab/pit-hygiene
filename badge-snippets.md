## Make the badge worth clicking

A badge that links to a pledge is fine. A badge that links to *your own* short note on how
each of the five commitments is met in *your* pipeline is much better — it turns a decoration
into documentation. Something like:

```markdown
[![PIT Hygiene](https://img.shields.io/badge/PIT%20Hygiene-pledged-2ea44f)](docs/pit-hygiene.md)
```

with `docs/pit-hygiene.md` answering the five commitments in five paragraphs.

## Wording

The badge reads **pledged**, and that is deliberate — it is a statement about what you commit
to, made by you. Please do not relabel it to anything implying an outside body checked your
pipeline; nobody did. If you want an outside look at a specific claim, that is what the
[audit registry](https://github.com/MaxWellApexLab/pit-audit-registry) is for.
